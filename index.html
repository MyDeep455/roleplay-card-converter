Those JanitorAI cards came through empty — here's why

JanitorAI hides every character definition from signed-out visitors, so the cards above have no greeting and no personality — only their public blurb, avatar and tags. It also limits you to the first page of results and refuses searches.

Adding your own JanitorAI token fixes all three. This page cannot grab it for you — browsers keep every site's cookies sealed off from every other site — but the helper below does it in one tap, and it is the only way that works on a phone.

One tap — works on phones too

  1. On a computer: drag Get my JanitorAI token onto your bookmarks bar.
    On a phone: press then bookmark any page, edit that bookmark, and replace its address with what you copied. Name it something you will recognise.
  2. Open janitorai.com and log in.
  3. Tap the bookmark while you are on that site. On a phone you may have to start typing its name in the address bar and pick it from the suggestions.
  4. Come back here, open Settings, and paste it into the JanitorAI box.

Or, on a computer, without the bookmark

  1. On janitorai.com, logged in, press F12.
  2. Go to Application → Cookies → https://janitorai.com.
  3. Copy the value of sb-auth-auth-token.0 (and .1 after it, if there is one — long logins get split in two).
  4. Paste it into Settings here and press Save.

The helper copies only your access token, never the rest of the cookie — the part it leaves behind is the one that could mint new logins. The token stays in your browser, is sent only to JanitorAI, and expires after a few hours, so repeat this when searches start failing. Cards whose author switched off “show definition” stay empty even with a token — roughly two in five.
